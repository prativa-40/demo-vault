# Hearth: The Household Intelligence Platform

## Executive summary

Hearth is a secure, AI-enabled household management platform that turns scattered family documents, records, bills, warranties, policies, and service history into one searchable source of truth. It gives a household a shared digital vault, structured records, proactive reminders, and answers to everyday questions such as: *When does our car insurance expire?* or *Where is the sale deed?*

The business begins with a familiar need—securely storing important family records—and expands into a trusted household operating system for life administration.

## The problem

Modern households manage a growing volume of fragmented information across phones, email inboxes, paper files, chat threads, and multiple family members. Critical records are difficult to locate when needed, expiry dates are missed, and ownership is unclear. A single person's phone cannot be a reliable household archive.

Existing cloud drives store files but do not understand them. Financial, medical, property, vehicle, education, and appliance information are usually managed in disconnected tools, if they are managed at all.

## The solution

Hearth provides a shared, permissioned household vault where users can:

- Capture or upload documents from web and mobile
- Extract key metadata automatically through OCR and AI-assisted classification
- Organise records by family member, property, vehicle, finance category, and household item
- Search in natural language and receive answers linked to source documents
- Track renewal, warranty, service, and expiry dates
- Share access safely across a household, with child and partner permissions
- Export or delete household data at any time

The home server is the canonical data store. Phones act as secure clients with optional local caches, so every member sees the same current information without any one phone becoming the source of truth.

## Target customers

Hearth is designed for digitally active households that value privacy, organisation, and continuity.

- Families managing children’s education, medical, identity, and financial records
- Homeowners with property documents, utility bills, appliances, warranties, and service contracts
- Multi-generational households coordinating responsibilities and shared assets
- Busy professionals seeking a simpler alternative to folders, spreadsheets, and reminder apps

| Module | Customer value |
|---|---|
| Family vault | Identity, education, health, and personal records per member |
| Home & property | Deeds, bills, appliances, warranties, and service contacts |
| Finance | Accounts, cards, deposits, insurance, and bulk Excel import |
| Vehicles | Registration, insurance, PUC, maintenance, and service documents |
| AI search | Natural-language answers with cited source records |
| Reminders | Alerts for insurance, passport, license, warranty, and service deadlines |
| Access controls | Household-head, homemaker, and child-level permissions |

## Differentiation

Hearth is not another file-storage product. Its differentiation is the combination of structured household records, privacy-first multi-user access, AI-assisted retrieval, and proactive reminders.

- **Search-first experience:** users ask questions instead of browsing folder trees.
- **Structured intelligence:** uploaded scans become usable records, dates, amounts, and reminders.
- **Household context:** data is organised around members and shared assets, rather than individual accounts only.
- **Privacy by design:** the household retains ownership and control of its data, with a clear export path.
- **Right-sized AI:** lower-cost extraction models handle routine work; higher-capability models are used only for complex questions.

## Business model

Hearth can use a subscription-led model with trust and privacy as its premium proposition.

| Tier | Indicative offer |
|---|---|
| Free | Limited documents, basic household vault, manual records |
| Household | Unlimited members, larger document allowance, OCR, reminders, and search |
| Hearth Plus | Advanced AI Q&A, extended history, priority support, and additional storage |
| Partner channel | Bundled offering through insurers, banks, home-management services, or employers |

Potential future revenue streams include premium storage, assisted onboarding and document digitisation, white-label partner integrations, and household concierge services.

## Go-to-market approach

The initial proposition should focus on a highly tangible customer promise: **“Never lose an important family document or miss an expiry date again.”**

1. Launch with family documents, home warranties, and renewal reminders.
2. Target homeowners and families through digital communities, property-management partners, and insurance or banking partnerships.
3. Use bulk Excel import and guided onboarding to reduce switching friction.
4. Introduce AI search after users have accumulated sufficient records.
5. Expand into tasks, recurring payments, emergency information, and household coordination.

## Technology and trust model

The platform uses encrypted document storage, a structured records database, and semantic search to make household information discoverable. A central home server stores the canonical data; client apps retrieve only the information each user is permitted to access.

Trust is central to the proposal. Production deployment should include encryption in transit and at rest, individual authentication, role-based access controls, audited document retrieval, secure backups, malware scanning, and transparent data export/deletion controls.

## Investment case

Hearth addresses a recurring, emotionally important consumer problem: keeping a family’s life administration secure, organised, and actionable. The initial product delivers clear utility through document retrieval and reminders. As the record base grows, AI search increases product value and makes Hearth harder to replace.

The opportunity is to become the trusted digital home for a household’s most important information—not merely a storage tool, but an intelligent, privacy-first platform that helps families manage everyday life with confidence.
